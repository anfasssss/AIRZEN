1. Project Executive Summary

AIRZEN is a multi-purpose, IoT-based smart healthcare and indoor environmental automation station powered by the ESP32 microcontroller. Operating seamlessly over Wi-Fi, the system continuously analyzes environmental health parameters—hazardous air pollutants, ambient noise levels, temperature, humidity, and fire hazards.

Unlike conventional monitoring setups, AIRZEN provides an integrated autonomous protection loop: it triggers an active Ventilation Fan during air pollution or excessive heat, closes an automated Noise-Containment Window using a micro servo when sound levels spike, and initiates a 5V Submersible Water Pump instantly upon flame detection while suppressing ventilation to prevent oxygen feeding the fire. Real-time telemetry is broadcast to an onboard high-tech 0.96" I2C OLED display and synchronized with a dedicated Custom Android Application (AIRZEN.apk) featuring audible siren alarms, push notifications, and remote controls for room lighting and door access.

2. Core Functional Subsystems

💨 Subsystem 1: Air Quality Monitoring & Automatic Ventilation (MQ-135)

The MQ-135 electrochemical sensor detects ammonia, benzene, smoke, and hazardous gases. When air quality falls below safe thresholds (ADC reading > 1500), the ESP32 automatically energizes Relay 1 to power on the Ventilation Fan, transmits a high-priority push notification to the user's smartphone, sounds an audible alarm on the phone, and displays live pollution metrics on both the app and OLED display. Built-in hysteresis turns the fan off when clean air levels normalize.

🔊 Subsystem 2: Acoustic Control & Automated Window Servo (Sound Sensor)

Ambient noise levels are captured in real-time. When sound spikes beyond the threshold (noise > 2400), Servo Motor 1 rotates to 90° to close the window, physically damping external noise. A phone alert and loud alarm notify the occupant. When ambient noise drops into prolonged silence, a low-sound alert is logged. Live sound intensity is streamed in real-time.

🌡️ Subsystem 3: Climate Regulation (DHT11 / DHT22 Sensor)

Monitors room temperature and humidity. If temperature crosses 35°C, a "Temperature High" message is dispatched, the Ventilation Fan turns ON, and the phone sounds an alarm. When humidity exceeds 75%, a "Humidity High" alert is triggered; as per design specifications, the ventilation fan is intentionally excluded for humidity.

🔥 Subsystem 4: Fire Emergency Suppression (Flame Sensor & 5V Water Pump)

Equipped with an infrared flame sensor. Upon fire detection, Relay 2 immediately activates a 5V Submersible Water Pump to disperse water throughout the target zone. Concurrently, the system activates a smartphone emergency siren, forces the ventilation fan OFF, and switches the OLED display into an inverted blinking emergency warning.

💡 Subsystem 5: Remote Actuator Controls (Light & Door Servo)

The custom Android app features dual interactive switches: one to toggle room illumination (LED on GPIO 25) and another to command Servo Motor 2 to lock or unlock the room door between 0° and 90°.

3. Hardware Pinout & Wiring Specification

4. Power Distribution & Common Ground Architecture

CRITICAL POWER RULE: Never power the SG90 servos, relays, and water pump from the ESP32 3.3V or VIN pins! Motor stall currents will cause brownouts and continuous microcontroller reboot cycles. Use an external 5V 2A–3A DC Power Adapter.
+------------------------------------------+ | EXTERNAL 5V 2A-3A DC POWER SUPPLY | +--------------------+---------------------+ | +---------------------------+---------------------------+ | (+5V VCC Rail) | (GND Rail) | | +---> SG90 Servo 1 (Window) VCC +---> Servo 1 GND +---> SG90 Servo 2 (Door) VCC +---> Servo 2 GND +---> 2-Channel 5V Relay VCC +---> 2-Channel Relay GND +---> 5V Submersible Pump (via Relay COM-NO) +---> Water Pump Negative (-) +---> MQ-135 Gas Sensor VCC +---> MQ-135 GND +---> ESP32 VIN (5V Input) +---> ESP32 GND <=== COMMON GROUND!
COMMON GROUND REQUIREMENT: The Ground (GND) wire of the external 5V power supply must connect directly to the ESP32 GND pin. Without this common electrical reference, PWM signals to servos and digital control signals to the relay coils will float and fail to switch reliably.

5. Custom Android App (AIRZEN.apk)

AIRZEN operates with a standalone, dedicated Native Android Application (compiled APK size: 5.4 MB). The application eliminates dependencies on third-party cloud brokers and connects directly to the ESP32 over local Wi-Fi.

📱 Key Mobile App Capabilities

  • Real-Time Visual Dashboard: Shows live telemetry cards for Air Quality, Sound Amplitude, Temperature, and Humidity.
  • Dynamic Acoustic Siren & Vibration: The app includes an internal audio synthesis engine (AlarmSoundManager) that generates a loud two-tone police emergency siren and triggers phone vibration upon threshold breach.
  • High-Priority Push Notifications: Utilizes Android's NotificationChannel.IMPORTANCE_HIGH to display Heads-Up emergency notifications even when the app is minimized.
  • Interactive Room Controls: Dedicated Material switches to instantly toggle the Room Light LED and Door Servo lock.
  • Auto-Discovery / Custom IP Config: Connects automatically to http://airzen.local via mDNS or allows manual IP entry.

How to Install & Connect the APK:

  1. Locate the compiled AIRZEN.apk in your project directory.
  2. Transfer AIRZEN.apk to your Android phone via USB cable, WhatsApp, Google Drive, or Bluetooth.
  3. Tap the file and select Install (enable "Allow from this source" if prompted).
  4. Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi network as the ESP32.
  5. Open the AIRZEN app, verify the IP shown on the OLED display (or use airzen.local), and tap Connect.

6. ESP32 Firmware & REST API Endpoints

The ESP32 firmware hosts an embedded HTTP REST server on port 80 with mDNS (http://airzen.local) and CORS headers.

Endpoint Method Parameters Response / Action
/api/data GET None Returns full JSON telemetry: air quality, temp, hum, sound, actuator states, and active alert.
/api/control POST / GET device=light&state=1|0 Switches Room Light LED ON or OFF.
/api/control POST / GET device=door&state=1|0 Rotates Door Servo to 90° (Open) or 0° (Locked).
/api/control POST / GET device=window&state=1|0 Manual override to open or close window servo.
/ GET None Serves the embedded mobile WebApp directly to browsers or WebViews.
